Engagement

How a project runs

No fixed price list — because a bakery's ordering system and a supplier's B2B platform are not the same project. Instead, a clear path from conversation to launch:

  1. Discovery call

    A free conversation about your business, what the software needs to do, and whether we're a fit.

  2. Requirements & scope

    I write up what we're building, what's included, what's explicitly not, and what it depends on.

  3. Proposal

    A fixed proposal with cost, milestones, and timeline — so there are no surprises later.

  4. Build milestones

    Work happens in visible stages with preview links, so you see the product as it forms, not just at the end.

  5. Launch

    Testing, accessibility and performance checks, production deployment, and handover.

  6. Support

    Optional ongoing maintenance and improvement — most of my client sites are living products, not one-off launches.

Straight answers

Common questions

Why custom instead of a template or site builder?

Templates are fine until your business doesn't fit them. Custom work costs more up front because it's built around your catalogue, your ordering patterns, and your customers — which is exactly what makes it convert better and last longer. If a template genuinely fits your needs, I'll tell you so on the discovery call.

What happens after launch?

You own the result. If you want ongoing help, we agree on a support arrangement for updates, content, performance, and new features. If you'd rather run it yourself, the handover includes what you need to do that.

Do you work in Tamil?

Yes — I build fully bilingual Tamil and English experiences, and several of my production builds are bilingual by design. I also work with clients comfortably in either language.

Can you take over or fix an existing website?

Yes. A technical audit is often the right first step: I review the current site's code, performance, SEO, and accessibility, and give you a written assessment before we decide whether to repair or rebuild.

What does a project cost?

It depends on scope, which is why the process starts with a free discovery call and ends with a fixed written proposal before any work begins. You'll never get a surprise invoice.
