How I work

A delivery process, not a guessing game

  1. Discover

    Understand the business, its customers, and what the software actually has to do. Requirements before pixels.

  2. Define

    Scope, milestones, and a technical plan you can read — what's included, what isn't, and what happens after launch.

  3. Design

    UX architecture and visual design focused on the people who will use it — customers, staff, and admins.

  4. Engineer

    Git-based implementation with preview deployments, so progress is visible as it happens.

  5. Test

    Functionality, responsiveness, accessibility, and performance — checked before anyone else sees it.

  6. Launch

    Production deployment, monitoring, and a clean handover with documentation.

  7. Improve

    Real products keep moving. Maintenance, iteration, and support after launch.
