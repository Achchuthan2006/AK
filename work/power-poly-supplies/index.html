01The problem

B2B supply is relationship commerce: customers buy in bulk, reorder on cycles, compare specifications, and need to trust that the right case of garment bags shows up when promised. Most suppliers this size run on phone calls and fax-era habits — which caps how many customers they can serve well.

The platform's job is to move that trust online: help a dry cleaner find the right product, understand sizing and quantities, get a price, and order — without losing the human relationship that B2B runs on.

02What the platform does

Product catalogue

Garment bags, polybags, and hangers organized by the way buyers actually shop — by business type and use case, not just category trees.

Bulk ordering & checkout

Case-quantity ordering with Square-backed checkout, built for repeat B2B purchasing.

Product education

Sizing guides, usage planners, and calculators that answer the questions buyers would otherwise phone in.

Industry pages

Tailored recommendations for dry cleaners, laundromats, retailers, and uniform services.

Quoting & sample requests

Structured contact and sample-request paths so bigger accounts can start the conversation the B2B way.

SEO groundwork

Discoverable product and industry pages, because B2B buyers start with a search too.

03Engineering meets operations

This project is unusual in my portfolio because I sit on both sides of it. In operations, I support B2B sales, order processing, invoicing, and wholesale account relationships. In engineering, I turn that daily reality into software — which is why the platform has calculators instead of brochures, and sample requests instead of a generic contact form.

  1. DiscoverA buyer lands on an industry page or product search and finds products matched to their business.
  2. EvaluateSizing guides and calculators answer the "which one, how many" questions on the page.
  3. Order or enquireCheckout handles standard orders; quoting and sample paths handle larger accounts.
  4. OperateOrders flow into the same operational routine I work in — processing, invoicing, fulfilment.

04Status & what's next

The platform is live at powerpolysupplies.com and actively maintained. Ongoing work focuses on deepening the ordering experience and the operational tooling behind it.

05What I learned

Working the counter changes how you write software. When you've processed the orders yourself, you stop building features and start removing phone calls — every guide, calculator, and quoting path on the site exists because I've answered that question for a customer in person.
