01The idea

Dessert is bought with the eyes. A dessert brand's website has one job before all others: make you want the thing. But most local dessert shops get a template with a phone number — no storytelling, no menu experience, no path from craving to order.

Sharon's is designed the other way around: brand first, appetite first, and a digital structure that can grow into ordering and multiple locations without being rebuilt.

02What I'm building

Premium brand direction

A visual identity and tone built for a dessert brand that wants to feel crafted, not generic.

Menu exploration

Menu discovery designed around browsing and craving, not a PDF scan.

Dessert-builder concept

A build-your-own experience that turns choosing toppings into part of the brand.

Mobile-first experience

Designed for the phone in line at the counter — where dessert decisions actually happen.

Media system

A structured approach to photography and product media so the brand stays consistent as it grows.

Franchise-ready structure

Locations, menus, and content modelled so expansion is configuration, not reconstruction.

03Status & what's next

The concept-and-build project is live as a development preview above. Next milestones include completing the menu experience, the dessert-builder prototype, and commerce readiness.

  • Brand system
  • Menu discovery
  • Dessert-builder concept
  • Mobile-first
  • Commerce-ready structure

04What I learned

Food brands taught me that design and commerce aren't separate phases — the menu structure IS the data model. Getting the brand experience and the underlying product data right together is what makes "add online ordering later" a small step instead of a rebuild.
