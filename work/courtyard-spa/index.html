01The problem

Service businesses that sell an experience are judged in the first three seconds of a website visit. If the site feels rushed or generic, the customer assumes the service will be too. For a spa, design isn't decoration — it's the first product demonstration.

Trust is the product — atmosphere, clarity, and an unhurried path to booking.

02The build's focus

Atmosphere-led design

Pacing, whitespace, and imagery direction that mirror the in-person experience.

Trust signals

Services, practitioners, and policies presented clearly — the details anxious first-timers look for.

Booking discovery

A clear path from browsing services to making a booking, without dead ends.

SEO for local intent

Structured so "spa near me" searches actually find it.
