Work/Rex Limo

A luxury chauffeur service deserves a luxury first impression.

Rex Limo is a limousine and chauffeur transportation business in the GTA. I'm building its premium digital presence: a black-and-gold brand identity, elegant vehicle and service presentation, and a clear path from first visit to reservation inquiry.

Client
Rex Limo — luxury transportation
Role
Brand, design, and engineering
Year

Status
In development
Luxury transportation · Greater Toronto Area Rex Limo Chauffeur service · fleet presentation · reservation journeys

01The problem

In luxury transportation, the website is the showroom. Customers booking wedding cars, corporate travel, or airport service judge the vehicle by the brand that presents it — and most limo sites undercut their own fleet with cluttered layouts, stock templates, and booking forms that feel like paperwork.

Rex Limo's digital presence has to do what the service itself does: feel effortless, look immaculate, and make the next step obvious.

The website is the showroom — a premium fleet deserves better than a template.

02What I'm building

Black-and-gold brand identity

A restrained luxury system — deep blacks, warm gold accents, generous negative space, and typography that carries the premium positioning.

Fleet & service presentation

Vehicles and service tiers presented like products — large imagery, clear capacities and occasions, no clutter.

Inquiry & reservation journeys

A low-friction path from browsing to a structured reservation inquiry — occasion, route, date, and party size captured cleanly.

Mobile-first usability

Most bookings start on a phone, often last-minute — the mobile experience is the primary experience.

Local SEO & discoverability

Structured data, service-area pages, and performance work so the business is findable for the searches that matter.

Lead-generation architecture

Every page composed to convert attention into contact — without feeling like a sales funnel.

03Approach

Luxury on the web is mostly restraint: fewer elements, better spacing, slower pacing, and photography given room to breathe. The design system is built dark-first around the fleet imagery, with gold used as a signal — never as wallpaper — and motion kept to quiet, confident transitions.

  1. ArriveA cinematic first impression establishes the brand and the standard of service.
  2. ExploreFleet and services presented by occasion — weddings, corporate, airport, events.
  3. InquireA structured reservation inquiry captures the details a dispatcher actually needs.
  4. Follow upInquiries arrive as clean, actionable requests the business can respond to fast.
  • Luxury brand system
  • Responsive design
  • Inquiry UX
  • Local SEO
  • Performance
  • Accessibility

04Status & what's next

The project is in active development. The rexlimo.com domain is reserved and currently shows a temporary holding page — not the new build — while the premium site is designed and engineered. Online reservation handling will be presented here only once it's verified and live.

05What this project exercises

Luxury service businesses are a different design problem from commerce or community platforms: the product is trust, and every pixel either adds to it or spends it. This build is where restraint, art direction, and conversion architecture have to work together — the same discipline this portfolio is built on.
