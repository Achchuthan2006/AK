01The challenge

Oviya's work already spoke for itself — on Instagram. But a feed is rented ground: no structure, no services, no way for a bride planning months ahead to understand what's offered, see the range of looks, and start a conversation. The business needed a brand home that matches the standard of the artistry.

The brief became: build a digital presence that feels like the work — considered, warm, and luxurious — and make the path from "I love this look" to a real enquiry as short as possible.

The portfolio already lived on Instagram — rented ground. The brand needed a home it owns.

02What I built

Cinematic brand experience

A dark, editorial identity with serif display typography and real photography given room to breathe — the site opens like a title sequence, not a template.

Three service disciplines

Makeup artistry, saree draping and pre-pleating, and hair styling — each presented as a craft with its own page and philosophy, not a price list.

Looks directory

Five art-directed starting points — from Natural & Minimal to Traditional Bridal — framing each as the start of a conversation rather than a package.

Editorial galleries

Portfolio and selected-work galleries built from real client photography, with category labels and numbering that read like a lookbook.

WhatsApp enquiry journeys

Every enquiry path leads somewhere real: a structured enquiry form, a one-tap WhatsApp link with a pre-filled message, phone, and Instagram DM.

Trust architecture

The artist's story, a "why Beauty Canvas" manifesto, client words from real Instagram comments, and an FAQ — the questions a bride actually asks, answered before she has to.

03Design direction

Beauty photography carries this site, so the interface stays out of its way: a near-black canvas with a warm plum undertone, ivory serif display type, gold used sparingly, and generous negative space. Motion is kept to quiet reveals — the photography provides the drama.

The voice mattered as much as the visuals. The site is written in the artist's own philosophy — "beauty in every shade," makeup that doesn't change your skin tone — so the brand reads as a person, not a service directory.

Portfolio galleries — real client work, presented like a lookbook.

04Engineering & discoverability

Under the photography, the build is disciplined: a mobile-first responsive system, accessible semantic structure with keyboard support and a skip link, and performance care so image-heavy pages stay fast. Deployed on Vercel at the brand's own domain.

  1. DiscoverLocal search positioning for Dehiwala–Colombo makeup queries — page titles, metadata, and content structured around how clients actually search.
  2. ExploreServices, looks, and galleries organized so a visitor can self-qualify before ever sending a message.
  3. EnquireStructured enquiry form plus one-tap WhatsApp with a pre-filled message — the channel Sri Lankan clients already use.
  4. ReturnInstagram and Facebook integration keeps the site and the feed pointing at each other.
  • Mobile-first responsive

05Status & what's next

The site is live in production at beautycanvasbyov.com, serving real enquiries for a working makeup artist. Ongoing work focuses on deepening the structured data and answer-engine optimization, expanding the galleries as new client work is photographed, and continuing performance tuning — the difference between launched and finished.
