About

An engineer who has worked both sides of the counter.

I'm Achchuthan Kumaranantha — a full-stack product engineer in the Greater Toronto Area, studying Computer Programming & Analysis at Humber College and building production software for real organizations while I do.

Most developers learn business requirements from a ticket. I learned them from processing wholesale orders, invoicing customers, and managing supplier relationships at Power Poly Supplies — a B2B packaging business where I also build and run the digital platform. That double view — operations by day, engineering underneath — is the core of how I work: software should match how a business actually runs, not how a template assumes it does.

The projects that followed came from communities I'm part of. A bilingual platform for my church, where I've volunteered since 2023. An artist site for a musician. Ordering systems for Tamil food businesses. A print-commerce engine. Each one is real: a real organization, real users, and the unglamorous work of keeping software alive after launch.

I work in English and Tamil — not just in conversation, but in the products themselves. Several of my builds are bilingual by design, which is rarer than it should be in a region where so many businesses serve multilingual communities.

Long term, I'm building toward product engineering at serious software companies — and toward a studio practice that keeps me close to the small businesses I started with. The two feed each other: client work keeps my engineering honest; engineering rigor keeps client work premium.
